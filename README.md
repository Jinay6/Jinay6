## Hi, I'm Jinay Patel

MSc Finance student at **EBS Universität** (Germany), with a BSc in Economics and Finance from **Shiv Nadar University** (India).

I work on **empirical asset pricing, financial econometrics and portfolio risk**: replicating published results,
testing whether they hold out of sample, and writing reproducible Python around them.

### Featured project

**[Volatility-Managed Portfolios](https://github.com/Jinay6/volatility-managed-portfolios)**: a replication of
Moreira & Muir (2017, *Journal of Finance*) on Fama-French factors, extended with ten years of post-publication data,
non-overlapping subsamples and a same-sample spanning test. Finding: the effect is concentrated in turbulent periods,
survives for momentum and profitability, and has faded since publication.
